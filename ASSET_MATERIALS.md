## Final prompt

Create a production game material texture atlas, square 2048x2048 image, four exactly equal 1024x1024 quadrants with no borders, no lettering, no logos, no objects, no lighting gradients, no perspective. Orthographic flat albedo-only scan of original fictional survival horror materials, extremely fine realistic detail, evenly diffuse-lit. TOP LEFT: pale desaturated diseased human skin close-up with subtle pores, mottling, bruised branching capillaries, old tiny scabs and scars, no large open wounds, no recognizable body part. TOP RIGHT: richly detailed dark brown diseased human skin with pores, fine wrinkles, small scar tissue and red-brown capillaries, no recognizable body part. BOTTOM LEFT: matte charcoal black phosphated gunmetal, extremely fine brushed machining marks, subtly rubbed gray edges throughout, tiny oil spots, realistic gritty microtexture, mostly uniform and low contrast, no orange rust. BOTTOM RIGHT: dense charcoal black feather surface, fine overlapping tapered feather barbs, dusty worn brown tips, natural organic detail, evenly distributed feather grain. Each quadrant is an independently tileable texture with matching opposite edges, consistent exposure, natural subdued colors. These will be UV mapped directly onto smooth 3D characters and firearms, not used as concept art. No highlights baked in, no drop shadows, no white seams.
