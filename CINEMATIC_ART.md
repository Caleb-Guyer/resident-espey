# Survivor textures and cinematic art

Built-in ImageGen mode was used. Originals remain in the generation directory; shipping assets were converted to 1024 × 1024 WebP with their transparency preserved. The generated faces are curved onto proportional 3D heads with nose relief, textured hair caps, and jointed arms.

Final shipping paths:
- `dist/assets/ethan-face.webp` — Ethan, blonde curly hair and blue eyes.
- `dist/assets/survivor-face.webp` — survivor facial albedo.

## Cinematic direction

Thirteen real-time scenes use original procedural sets: the CS sanctuary, survivor supply closet, serum station, Oak Village road, Backrooms, Geographer’s Seal, Room 204 breach, and a sunrise escape. Cameras move through the actual scene; actors have positional blocking and poses. Letterboxing, restrained captions, door slams, heartbeat cues, collapse noise, and a final resolving chord support the action. Expanded notebook prose is optional.

The menu uses a quiet vertical list over the existing cover. The HUD keeps vitals and ammunition small, fades objectives, and frames interaction prompts close to the object. No marketing cards or forced notebook dialogs remain.
